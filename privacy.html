Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

This policy explains what clipfarm, the private publishing tool run by chat lost it, accesses and how we handle it.

What we access

clipfarm only connects to our own accounts. It never accesses anyone else's account and does not collect information about viewers or the public. From our own accounts it uses only:

  • Basic account information, such as the account name and ID, to make sure we post to the right account.
  • Permission to upload videos to our own YouTube channel, Instagram account and TikTok account.
  • View counts and basic statistics of our own posts, so we can see how they perform.

How we keep it safe

Access tokens are stored securely on our private server, readable only by our own account. They are never put in code, never shown in logs, and never shared.

We never sell or share data

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising. It is used only to post our own videos and to see how they perform.

Google and YouTube

clipfarm uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to our own channel and read their basic statistics. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. clipfarm's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page.

How long we keep it

Access tokens are kept until we disconnect the account. Video files are kept on our server only as long as needed to post them. Post links and view counts are kept for our own records.

Deletion and questions

To ask a question or to ask us to delete any data, email govindasamymidhun@gmail.com. We will reply and delete the data within 30 days. Access can also be removed at any time in the security settings of Google, Meta (Instagram) or TikTok.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
